Review state treatments

Borrowed from Direction B

  • Light surfaces, crisp type and generous public whitespace.
  • Calm navigation, direct calls to action and soft elevation.
  • Strong form and verification-result hierarchy.

Borrowed from Direction A

  • Dark, compact workspace shell with persistent navigation.
  • Monospace metadata for IDs, timestamps, sequences and digests.
  • Dense tables, source tiles and a timeline-led incident view.

Product-specific constraints

  • Status always includes text; colour is secondary.
  • Unknown and unavailable states are neutral gray, not red.
  • Platform probe is always labelled platform-operated.
  • No confidence scores, claims of cause or decorative dashboards.
